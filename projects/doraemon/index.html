K11 Guangzhou · Exhibition & audience experience

K11 — Doraemon Exhibition & Brand Experience

Connecting exhibition spaces, merchandise displays, and audience participation at the Guangzhou tour.

Exhibition installationSpatial coordinationVisitor experienceVisual merchandisingDisplay designEvent support

Overview

I contributed to the installation and spatial coordination of the K11 100% Doraemon & FRIENDS touring exhibition in Guangzhou, helping support a cohesive visitor experience.

Exhibition & spatial coordination

I assisted with exhibition setup and on-site layout refinement, tracked progress across teams, and supported timely adjustments. My role focused on spatial execution and coordination within a collaborative project.

Display & audience engagement

This work extended the exhibition experience beyond viewing into interaction and participation through merchandise displays and audience-facing activities. I selected products and developed visual arrangements for display cases. I also supported weekly events and on-site engagement through hosting, organization, and coordination.
